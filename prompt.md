# 🚀 Personal Portfolio Website Design (Software Developer)

Design a **modern, fun, interactive, and memorable personal portfolio website** for a **Software Developer** that showcases my profile, technical expertise, professional experience, and real-world projects.

The website should feel highly personal and storytelling-driven while maintaining a polished and professional appearance.

Visitors should immediately understand:

- Who I am
- What I build
- What technologies I work with
- My professional journey
- Why they should collaborate or hire me

The design should be created in **Figma first**, following modern SaaS, startup, and award-winning portfolio design trends.

---

# 🎯 Main Goal

Create a portfolio website that feels:

- Personal
- Modern
- Playful
- Professional
- Memorable

The experience should combine:

- Strong personal branding
- Modern UI design
- Interactive elements
- Storytelling
- Clear presentation of projects and skills

The overall impression should be:

> "This developer is skilled, creative, approachable, and capable of building high-quality digital products."

---

## Hero Layout Structure

Create a full-screen hero section (`100vh`).

### Top Navigation

#### Left

```text
🟢 Available for New Opportunities
```

#### Center

```text
Work
Projects
Experience
Contact
```

#### Right

```text
Let's Talk →
```

Navigation should feel minimal, elegant, and lightweight.

---

## Oversized Name Typography

Place my name as a massive background element behind the portrait.

Example:

```text
JOHN DOE
```

Typography treatment:

### First Name

- Outline only
- Transparent fill
- Large scale

### Last Name

- Solid fill
- Bold
- High contrast

The typography should occupy most of the hero width and sit behind the portrait.

This is a major visual element.

---

## Portrait Placement

Place a large professional portrait image at the center of the hero section.

### Requirements

- Cutout image with transparent background
- Positioned in front of the large typography
- Extends toward the bottom of the viewport
- Main focal point of the page
- Premium lighting
- Soft shadow underneath

The portrait should immediately establish a personal connection with visitors.
